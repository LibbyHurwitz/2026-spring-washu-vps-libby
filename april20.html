One thing I long for from the past is my iPod nano from when I was 6 years old. It may seem like a bit of an odd thing to long for but growing up as the oldest, my parents always read me a bed time story. That was until my younger brother and sister came along and no longer had time to read be to bed every night so they got me an iPod nano that had all of my favorite audiobooks downloaded on it so I could play it at night to help me sleep. Overtime I accumulated more than 20 audiobooks on it as I learned to read and my taste developed. In addition to audiobooks this iPod also had lots of pictures which I could scroll through if I couldn’t fall asleep or woke up from a bad dream. Me and my brother named it “Bluey” rather unimaginatively after its blue color. Its odd to thing this little metal square no bigger than 2 inches in width was such an integral part of my childhood every night for almost 6 consistent years. We moved houses when I was 10 and somewhere within the chaos of shipping everything, Bluey was misplaced. All of the audiobooks I loved were lost and while I could redownload them on another device, at that point I was old enough that I didn’t need stories to fall asleep at night and so it was never replaced. While I don’t have need for that iPod nano now, I miss that time of innocence in my life where something now so outdated could have consistently brought me joy.
