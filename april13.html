The man-made object that allows me to be the best version of myself is my AirPods. That sounds a bit generic but for me music is one of the most important things in my life and the ability to tune out the rest of the world for even 3 minutes at a time and lose myself in a song is what allows me to be the best version of myself. I’m the kind of person where my mind is always racing and my thoughts never settle. I used to struggle a lot falling asleep for a while because I could never turn my brain off for long enough to fall asleep. When I hear a good song I am instantly captivated and immersed into another world. Some people detest the idea of listening to music while they work but for me it allows me to do my best work because by focusing on the tune, im able to tune-out everything else and prioritize what is in front of me. Obviously AirPods are not the only way I can listen to music but I find it interesting that they are one of the only ways to achieve a sense of complete individuality and privacy while in an otherwise completely public space.
