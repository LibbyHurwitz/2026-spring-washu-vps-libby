I found this documentary to be incredibly profound and conceptually provoking. The documentary’s generative structure perfectly mirrors Eno’s philosophy that complexity arises from simplicity, and it is our job as the viewer to piece the ingredients together to create the final product. As someone who plays musical instruments in addition to creating art, I love how the documentary reframes the human desire for music as the desire for synchronicity. This made me think about how it’s similar to the Internet in that the internet is an endless collection of fragments that only become coherent through the way we interact with them. I was really impacted by the idea of music and art as a biological process where the focus is on the environment in which it grows as opposed to a final polished product. He mentioned a “no buts” approach when in the beginning stages of creating something which I will definitely adopt into my own practice as we are our own harshest critics. I also think it’s super cool that he made the Microsoft Windows startup sound.
