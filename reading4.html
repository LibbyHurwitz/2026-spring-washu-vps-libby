This essay was a very new and refreshing perspective for me, as it forced me to think about websites in a way I hadn’t before. I’ve always thought of a website as merely a digital expression of a physical goal or idea with a specific function. This article really broadened my perspective on what a website can be by framing it instead as an evolving space rather than a static object. I especially liked the idea that a website creator is both author and architect simultaneously, because it gives the creator a more intentional role in shaping the content and the experience of the site itself. I also resonated with the simile of a website as a puddle. Like a puddle, a website can change in size, clarity, and purpose over time.The passage about puddles being ephemeral, yet still capable of supporting life, helped me see value in websites that don’t last forever. Not everything needs permanence to be meaningful.
